EZ-Reader test fixtures

These fixtures are served from the local fixture root and loaded by the headless acceptance suite as /fixtures/<name>.html. Every page is a complete, self-contained HTML5 document that renders with zero network access: system font stacks only, no external stylesheets, no external scripts, no images loaded over the network, and no fonts downloaded at runtime. Nothing on any page mutates the DOM on its own, so serialising document.documentElement.outerHTML before and after activating the reader must produce a byte-identical result.

Main region ids are stable and deliberately memorable: #article for the article-shaped fixtures and #content for the div-soup fixture. Each page also carries <meta name="ezr-fixture" content="<name>"> in the head and a matching data-ezr-fixture attribute on the body.

Fixtures

Fixture, main region id, and the behaviour each one exists to exercise.
Fixture Main region What it tests
paper.html#articleSemantic academic structure: h1 title, six h2 sections, nested h3 and h4, 20 dense paragraphs of 60–140 words, a blockquote, a pre/code block, and a figure with a caption but no image file.
divsoup.html#contentDiv soup with no heading tags at all: levels come only from 30px / 21px / 17px font sizes, body text is 16px at line-height 1.35 with a 4px paragraph gap, and short byline and date divs must not be promoted.
rich.html#articleInline formatting and exclusion: every whitelisted inline element, links, a bare URL, an e-mail address, lists, a 4-column table, a data-URI image, a script that must be stripped, malicious links that must degrade to text, and hidden text that must be dropped.
long.html#articlePerformance: exactly 5000 static paragraph-level blocks (4950 paragraphs and 50 headings, one heading every 100th block) with no runtime generation, no timers, and no external resources.
lazy-iframe.html#articleRobustness: an srcdoc iframe, a fixed bottom bar, overflow: hidden on html and body with a scrollable inner wrapper that must be restored on exit, and collapsed details content that must not be extracted.
cjk.html#articleChinese content: one h1, three h2 sections and ten paragraphs of Chinese prose, with exactly one embedded English sentence so mixed-content handling and the CJK-dominance warning can be asserted.

Per-fixture notes

  • paper.html
    main region: id="article"
    Use this fixture for heading-level assignment, paragraph rhythm, and noise rejection around a long academic article. The chrome regions sit outside the article: a masthead, a site nav, and a three-column footer of related articles, sharing links, and comments.
  • divsoup.html
    main region: id="content"
    The hard case. Inside #content there are no semantic tags and no class or id names containing navigation, menu, comment, footer, share, related, promo, advert, cookie, banner, toolbar, sidebar, or breadcrumb hints, so the noise filter has nothing to latch onto inside the article. Those words appear only on the genuine chrome: the masthead, the toolbar strip, the main menu, the breadcrumb, the sidebar promo and advert slots, and the footer.
  • rich.html
    main region: id="article"
    Keep this one for sanitizer and title-case coverage. The article contains the marker strings EZR_SHOULD_BE_STRIPPED_1, EZR_SHOULD_BE_STRIPPED_2, and EZR_HIDDEN_TEXT, plus casing traps such as iPhone, DNA, U.S.A., e-mail, state-of-the-art, and 2024, and two links whose destinations are scripts rather than documents.
  • long.html
    main region: id="article"
    A static file of roughly 1.4 MB holding exactly 5000 paragraph-level blocks, counted by counting occurrences of the ezr-block class inside #article: 4950 paragraphs plus 50 headings. Use it to check scan time, the 20000-block ceiling, and outline construction at scale.
  • lazy-iframe.html
    main region: id="article"
    Loads nothing from the network: the iframe carries its whole four-paragraph card in a srcdoc attribute. The page also sets overflow: hidden on both html and body and scrolls an inner wrapper instead, so the reader must record and restore the original overflow value on exit. The collapsed details element holds EZR_COLLAPSED_TEXT, which must not appear in the reading view while it is closed.
  • cjk.html
    main region: id="article"
    Ten paragraphs of Chinese prose, roughly 150 to 190 Han characters each, with one English sentence of more than fifteen words inside the sixth paragraph. Capitalising the first letter of every word must leave the Chinese text untouched, and the CJK-dominance warning is expected to fire on this page.
