怎样导入 Anki？

下载后在 Anki 中选择“文件 → 导入”，选中导出的 txt 文件。笔记类型选择双面（Basic），两列分别映射到正面和背面。文件已包含制表符、HTML 与 EZReader 标签设置。

通用 TSV 可用于其他记忆软件，或复制到 Quizlet 的导入框：词语和释义以制表符分隔，每行一张卡片。扩展不会自动上传卡片。

首字母大写

只影响拉丁字母文字（英文等）：中日韩文字没有大小写，开启后对中文页面没有任何视觉变化。 这是渲染期变换，不会改动网页原文，关闭后立即恢复原样；每个词只把首字符转成大写， 其余字符保持原样，所以 iPhone、DNA 这类写法不会被破坏；纯数字、网址（URL）与邮箱地址会被跳过。

Riverfront plan clears the council after a decade of stalemate

The city council voted on Tuesday to approve a sweeping rezoning plan that would allow developers to build as many as nine thousand homes along the riverfront, ending a decade of stalemate over the future of the old freight yards. Officials said the agreement, reached after eleven hours of negotiation, would set aside nearly a third of the site for parks and a new elementary school.

Not everyone was pleased. Residents of the adjacent mill district argued that the plan's affordable housing quota, set at eighteen percent, is far too low for a neighbourhood where median rents have climbed by more than half since 2019. "We are not against growth," one longtime owner told the chamber. "We are against growth that pushes us out."

Construction is expected to begin next spring, though two lawsuits filed last month could delay the first phase by a year or more. City planners concede that the timetable is optimistic, but they point to a similar project across the river that broke ground within eighteen months of approval and now houses some four thousand people.
